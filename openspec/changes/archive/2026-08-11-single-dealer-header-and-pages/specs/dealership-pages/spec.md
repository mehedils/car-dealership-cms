## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Dedicated Services Page
The system SHALL provide a dedicated Services page at `/services` displaying dealership service offerings using the `Service` model data.

#### Scenario: User visits Services page
- **WHEN** a user navigates to `/services`
- **THEN** the platform SHALL render the Services page listing all dealership services with titles, descriptions, and images.

### Requirement: Dedicated About Us Page
The system SHALL provide a dedicated About Us page at `/about` displaying dealership background, Why Choose Us features, team members, and testimonials.

#### Scenario: User visits About page
- **WHEN** a user navigates to `/about`
- **THEN** the platform SHALL render the About page displaying company history, why choose us features, team members, and testimonials.
