## Requirements
### Requirement: Category Card Circular Arrow Button Styling
The system SHALL style category card arrow buttons (`.card-popular .card-button a`) with centered flex alignment and crisp white arrow icon contrast (`#ffffff`) on hover over primary theme background (`var(--bs-brand-2)`).

#### Scenario: User hovers over a category card
- **WHEN** a user hovers over a category card or its arrow button
- **THEN** the arrow button background SHALL transition to primary brand theme color (`var(--bs-brand-2)`), and the arrow icon SHALL display in crisp white (`#ffffff`).
